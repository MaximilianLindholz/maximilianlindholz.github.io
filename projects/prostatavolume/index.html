PROSTATE VOLUME

Three orthogonal diameters, measured on TRUS or T2-weighted MRI, give the volume of a prolate ellipsoid. Enter them and the result updates as you type.

Measurements in

— ml

PSA ng/ml  →  density — ng/ml²

V = L × W × H × π/6, with π/6 ≈ 0.52. The ellipsoid formula overestimates slightly in small glands and underestimates in large ones; planimetry on MRI remains the reference standard.

PSA density is serum PSA divided by volume. A threshold of 0.15 ng/ml² is commonly used to stratify risk, and PI-RADS 3 lesions below 0.10 are often managed without immediate biopsy.

1 ml of prostate tissue weighs approximately 1 g, so volume in ml and weight in g are used interchangeably.
